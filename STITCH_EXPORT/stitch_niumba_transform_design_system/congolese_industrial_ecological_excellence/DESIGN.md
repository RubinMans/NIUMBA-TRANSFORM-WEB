## Brand & Style

This design system establishes the visual and operational identity for an innovative industrial enterprise and its premier household hygiene brand. The visual language blends rigorous scientific engineering with approachable, vibrant consumer product packaging. 

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Industrial Pan-African Leadership:** Confident, structured, and modern; reflecting sovereign manufacturing capability and local value transformation with international technical standards.
- **Eco-Conscious Vitality:** Grounded in sustainable chemistry, natural freshness, and public health empowerment. Cleanliness is presented not as a sterile chore, but as community pride and daily well-being ("La propreté qui nous ressemble").
- **Commercial Accessibility:** Crisp, straightforward purchasing workflows pairing direct e-commerce affordances with high-touch conversational commerce (WhatsApp ordering, regional distribution pipelines).

### Visual Aesthetic & Movement
The design adopts a **Modern Corporate & Crisp Editorial** style infused with vibrant product color accents:
- Generous pristine white backdrops `#FFFFFF` paired with cool neutral surfaces `#F8FAFC`.
- Rich structural depth driven by a deep forest green corporate spine `#0E4B2A` and punchy emerald actions `#008751`.
- Fully rounded pill interactions (`rounded-full`) housing circular micro-icon containers that indicate progress and forward momentum.
- Editorial card structures framing vivid photographic packshots with soft container borders and precise botanical/functional badges.

## Colors

The color system delivers high visual hierarchy between institutional credibility (deep corporate forest tones) and high-visibility retail utility (luminous emerald, sun-drenched citrus yellow, and clear category badges).

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0E4B2A`):** Deep Forest Green. Serves as the anchor for headers, primary CTAs, institutional banners, and structural dark-mode highlights (video showcase sections and global footers).
- **Secondary (`#008751`):** Vivid Emerald. Used for primary interactive triggers, active tabs, inline hover states, and dynamic visual indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Solar Lemon Accent. Sourced directly from citrus formulation packaging; highlights product badges, promotional tags, and conversion accents.
- **WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`):** Dedicated utility color reserved strictly for conversational ordering triggers, chat buttons, and direct dispatch links.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFFFF` (pure white canvas for product isolation and crisp hero segments).
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F8FAFC` (cool stone slate background for product grid backing and secondary cards).
  - `Text Primary`: `#0F172A` (deep charcoal black ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against white and mint).
  - `Text Secondary`: `#475569` (slate grey for technical descriptors, subtitles, and metadata).
  - `Border Soft`: `#E2E8F0` (ultra-clean divider line for containment cards and tabular dividers).
- **Thematic Domain Badges:**
  - *Entreprise (Corporate & Industry):* `#0E4B2A` background with `#E8F5E9` soft tint.
  - *Environnement (Ecology & Recycling):* `#008751` background with `#D1FAE5` soft tint.
  - *Emploi & Social (Workforce & Training):* `#1D4ED8` deep cobalt with `#EFF6FF` soft tint.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to reflect a clean, modern, technological yet human spirit. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines (`display-hero`, `headline-xl`):** Set in 800 (Extra Bold) or 700 (Bold) with deliberate negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). This imparts authority, punch, and clarity across French industrial marketing headers.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Set at weight 400 with open, balanced line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) in Slate `#475569` to prevent fatigue during longer institutional readings (R&D descriptions, mission statements).
- **Action & Labels (`label-lg`, `label-md`):** Set in weight 600 or 700 with subtle letter spacing for uppercase or semi-condensed button labels, navigation links, and packaging volumes (`1L`, `250 ml`).
- **Brand Slogan Script Accent:** The brand motto *"La propreté qui nous ressemble"* is treated as an editorial flourish, ideally rendered via an expressive handwritten or angled script element positioned near flagship hero bottles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **12-column responsive grid** bounded within a maximum container width of `1280px` for optimal viewing on desktop monitors, down-scaling smoothly for tablets and mobile devices.

### Grid & Responsiveness Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Section vertical cadence uses `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to `4rem` spacing to maintain breathing room between category rails, company narrative cards, and interactive video bays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1rem` gutters. Product cards reflow from 5 columns down to a 3-column or 2-column wrapping arrangement.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) outer canvas margins. Category pill collections and video modules collapse into touch-friendly horizontal swipe corridors with snap points.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Compact inner padding (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) is strictly applied to status chips, badges, and icon buttons.
- Medium inner padding (`space-md`, `space-lg`) structures the internal real estate of product cards, callout boxes, and hero banner cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines **crisp planar boundaries** with **luminous green-tinted ambient glows** rather than traditional heavy charcoal shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` base without shadows.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Category Pills):** Low-contrast borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) with an ultra-soft atmospheric shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hovered Product Cards & Floating Triggers):** Subtle elevation with a vibrant emerald warmth: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(14, 75, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#D1FAE5`.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation & Conversational Floating Actions):** Clean backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` and a soft perimeter shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`.
5. **Level 4 (Hero Contrast Blocks & Media Players):** Immersive inverted dark panels (`#0E4B2A` to `#062816` gradient) with deep rim illumination and subtle glassmorphic video thumbnails.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Pill & Smooth Softness (Level 3)** paradigm for controls, contrasted with clean, moderately rounded geometric containers (`12px` to `16px`) for cards and frames.

### Corner Radius System
- **Pill Shape (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** The signature shape for all call-to-action buttons, quick filter category buttons, search inputs, and circular icon wrappers (e.g., arrow disks inside CTA buttons).
- **Cards & Media Containers (`16px` / `rounded-2xl`):** Applied to product showcases, blog cards, factory video frames, and partnership callouts.
- **Category Thumbnail Squares (`12px` / `rounded-xl`):** Applied to quick category selectors (e.g., Savons, Vaisselle, Lessive) featuring soft `#F1F5F9` background tiles.
- **Badges & Micro Pills (`rounded-full`):** Category pills and metadata indicators use pill radii with compact horizontal padding.

## Components

### 1. Navigation Header
- **Structure:** Clean white bar with top-level corporate links, quick search trigger, mini-cart preview, green CTA button ("Commander"), and national market selector featuring the DRC flag (`Made in DRC`) and language picker (`FR`).
- **Brand Identity:** Company logo lockup (`Niumba Transform`) on the left, high-contrast links with green bottom-indicator bars on active state.

### 2. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Pill Button:** Solid `#0E4B2A` or `#008751` background with white text, fully rounded (`rounded-full`). Includes an embedded trailing circular icon disk (`#008751` or white-tinted) holding a forward directional arrow (`→`).
- **Secondary Outline Button:** Border `1.5px solid #008751`, transparent background, dark text `#0E4B2A`, with smooth background fill transition on hover (`#E8F5E9`).
- **WhatsApp Quick-Order Button:** Pill button with distinct WhatsApp green (`#25D366`), white icon, and bold white label for instant customer conversion.

### 3. Product Cards
- **Base Canvas:** White card surrounded by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `rounded-2xl` corners.
- **Asset Showcase:** Isolated product bottle image placed centered on a neutral or soft mint gradient stage (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Typography & Details:** Product family tag in bold uppercase label (`BUKHETE`), product subtitle (`Vaisselle`, `Savon liquide mains`), and distinct packaging measure (`250 ml`, `1 L`, `2 kg`) in muted slate `#64748B`.
- **Interaction:** Hover lifts the card slightly with a soft emerald halo and exposes immediate add-to-cart or view-spec action.

### 4. Category Navigators (Icon Tiles)
- Centered vertical layout: soft rounded square (`rounded-2xl`) with light neutral backdrop `#F1F5F9`, displaying the isolated bottle packshot or icon, followed underneath by bold category title (`Savons`, `Vaisselle`, `Lessive`, `Désinfection`).

### 5. Thematic Badges & Chips
- Embedded pill tags on article cards and product descriptions:
  - *Entreprise*: Solid `#0E4B2A` badge with crisp white sans-serif.
  - *Environnement*: Vibrant `#008751` badge with white text.
  - *Emploi*: Vibrant royal blue `#1D4ED8` badge with white text.

### 6. Media & Video Feature Frame
- Immersive forest green backdrop (`#0E4B2A`) enclosing a large prominent video preview frame with centered translucent play button (`rgba(255,255,255,0.2)` backdrop blur).
- Paired with an interactive vertical playlist featuring video thumbnail snippets, runtime stamps (`1:25`, `1:30`), and quick-launch triggers.

### 7. Global Footer
- Deep industrial forest background (`#0A341D`) featuring corporate certification details (RCCM, Id.Nat, NIF), comprehensive sitemap links in muted green-grey, social channel pills, DRC origin acknowledgment, and a circular scroll-to-top button.
